What is SereneMeds?

SereneMeds is an online pharmacy and health marketplace where you can order medicines, wellness products, and everyday healthcare essentials, then have them delivered straight to your door.

Do you only sell medicine?

Not at all. Alongside medicines, we carry beauty care, sports nutrition, supplements, personal care, and mother & baby products.

How is SereneMeds different from others?

We combine licensed pharmacist support, fast delivery, and transparent pricing, so you always know what you're getting and when it will arrive.

Can pharmacies & hospitals use your services?

Yes, we offer bulk ordering and dedicated support for pharmacies, clinics, and hospitals. Reach out to our business team to get set up.

Does SereneMeds provide pharmacist Counseling?

Absolutely. Our licensed pharmacists are available to answer questions about dosage, interactions, and general medication guidance.

Do you provide any free test services?

Select locations offer complimentary basic health screenings, such as blood pressure and glucose checks. Availability varies by branch.

Where is SereneMeds located?

Our corporate office is at Marasi Dr, Business Bay, Bay Square, with delivery partners covering multiple cities.

How do I cancel my order?

Go to My Orders in your account, select the order, and tap Cancel. Orders already out for delivery can't be cancelled online, contact support instead.

How can I place my order?

Browse or search for a product, add it to your cart, upload a prescription if required, and check out with your preferred payment method.

How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll get a tracking link by SMS and email. You can also check live status under My Orders.

Can I change my order details? (address/items)

You can edit your address or items within 30 minutes of placing an order, after that, please contact our support team for help.

Do you have any stores across Pakistan?

Yes, we have physical branches in major cities. Use our Store Locator to find the one closest to you.

How do I upload my prescription?

During checkout, tap Upload Prescription and choose a clear photo or PDF. Our pharmacist will verify it before dispatch.
